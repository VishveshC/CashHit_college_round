## Plan finding

1. **Readback and historical timer seam — resolved before implementation by A-26.** The original plan left A-24's progress fields without an explicit getter contract. A-26 now appends `submittedAt`, `reviewEnd`, `rejectedAt`, `responseEnd`, `rejectionCount`, `submissionCount`, and `latestReasonCommitment` to `MilestoneView`; it also makes rejection time, response end, and reason commitment historical after resubmission or settlement. ## Required adversarial proof when implementation lands

- **Half-open windows and roles:** For submission/grace, approval and rejection/review, resubmission/response, and their permissionless timeout calls, exercise `E-1`, `E`, and `E+1`. At `E`, the actor action must fail and the timeout must succeed. Also prove wrong callers and wrong states cannot change a milestone, job state, or ledger.
- **Revision accounting and history:** Cover limits 0, 1, and 5 across more than one milestone. A rejection consumes exactly one revision, resubmission does not consume another, a later rejection cannot reset the counter, and one milestone's rejections do not affect another. Version 1 is the first submission; version/history length never exceeds `revisionLimit + 1` (at most 6). Readback must show immutable earlier records and reject an unknown job or out-of-range submission index.
- **Resubmission after original grace:** Reject a valid submission, advance past the original `graceEnd` but remain before `responseEnd`, and prove the freelancer can resubmit. Verify the new review end is derived from the resubmission timestamp, not the original submission or grace deadline.
- **Rightful one-time credit and closure:** For every settlement trigger, assert the full milestone amount moves once from `lockedEscrow` to only the rightful party's per-token claim and total claimable. Attempts to repeat or cross-settle a terminal milestone must revert without changing balances. In a multi-milestone job, settle different milestones to both parties and prove the job stays `Funded` until the last milestone becomes terminal, then becomes `Closed` in that same transaction.
- **No external token dependency in settlement:** After funding, make the test token's external methods fail or change its runtime code, then settle by approval and by timeout. Both settlements must still credit the ledger and reach terminal/closed states without an external token call. Keep withdrawal's existing exact-transfer and codehash checks. A successful settlement must never rely on the Phase 1 fixture credit helper.
- **Bounded exits and non-vacuity:** Show each implemented nondisputed nonterminal state (`Ready`, `Submitted`, `Rejected`) has a callable public timeout once its own window ends, and show the exercised handler actually creates and exits each state. Do not describe this as proving exits for `Disputed`; those actions and arbitration remain Phase 3.
- **Frozen Phase 1 boundary:** All frozen Phase 1 files and their integrity evidence must remain unchanged and pass. Constructor/config, create/accept/fund, cancellation, withdrawal, and existing readback behavior must remain compatible.

These are review criteria, not claims that the current tests already prove them. Dispute deposits, arbitration, no-quorum behavior, Tier B F-23 deadline changes, UI behavior, and production readiness are outside this review.
